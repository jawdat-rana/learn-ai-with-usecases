# News snippets (synthetic)

These eight short "news stories" are made up for the exercise. Every company
name is fictional. Some stories matter, some don't, and at least one is a trap.

1. **Shipping outlook.** Forecasters expect a stronger-than-usual storm season
   near major East Asian ports in the first quarter, with possible closures of
   two to five days at a time.

2. **Voltaria Cells to consolidate plants.** Battery maker Voltaria Cells will
   close one of its three cell plants and move production to the other two by
   the end of Q2. In a letter to customers it warned of "temporary allocation"
   during the move.

3. **Nimbus Semicon misses bond payment.** Chip packager Nimbus Semicon did not
   make a scheduled interest payment this week. The company says talks with
   lenders are "constructive" and production continues as normal.

4. **Kestrel Metalworks wins aerospace contract.** Kestrel Metalworks has
   signed a five-year contract with an aerospace customer and plans to grow
   capacity by 40% over the next 18 months.

5. **Strike vote at Lumen Textiles.** Workers at Lumen Textiles' main mill
   will vote next month on strike action over pay. Management says
   contingency plans are in place.

6. **Northwind Circuit Solutions fined.** Regulators fined Northwind Circuit
   Solutions, a small printed-circuit repair shop, for waste-disposal
   violations at its only site.

7. **New export licences for battery cells.** A new rule requires an export
   licence for lithium battery cells from next quarter. Officials expect
   approvals to take four to eight weeks at first.

8. **Polyform launches recycled resin.** Polyform Ltd has launched a resin
   grade made from 50% recycled material, at a price similar to its standard
   grade.

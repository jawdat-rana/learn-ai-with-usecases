**3. Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs for 36
months. It renews automatically for further 24-month periods unless either
party gives written notice at least 180 days before the end of the
current term.

**5. Charges.** 5.1 Charges are set out in Schedule 2. 5.2 Provider may
adjust the charges once per year by giving 30 days' notice, to reflect
changes in its costs. 5.3 Fuel, energy and labour surcharges may be applied
at any time where Provider's costs rise by more than 3%.

**7. Service levels.** 7.1 Provider will use reasonable endeavours to meet
the KPIs in Schedule 3, including order accuracy of 99.5% and dispatch of
98% of orders received before 14:00 on the same day. 7.2 KPI performance is
measured and reported monthly by Provider. 7.3 If a KPI is missed in three
consecutive months, the parties will meet to agree an improvement plan.

**8. Service credits.** Where order accuracy falls below 98.5% in a month,
Provider will credit 2% of that month's charges. Service credits are the
Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the KPIs and are
capped at 5% of annual charges.

**10. Stock.** 10.1 Provider is liable for loss of or damage to stock while
in its custody, up to a maximum of $5 per kilogram. 10.2 Stock counts take
place annually. Shrinkage up to 1.5% of throughput is accepted without
charge to Provider.

**12. Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for failure caused by events
beyond its reasonable control, including labour shortages, strikes by
Provider's own staff, IT system failure and supplier delays.

**14. Data.** All operational data generated by Provider's systems in
performing the services, including inventory and order history, is the
property of Provider. Customer may request a copy on termination, at
Provider's then-current rates.

**15. Termination.** Customer may terminate for material breach on 90 days'
notice if the breach is not remedied. On termination, Customer pays an exit
fee equal to 6 months of average charges.
